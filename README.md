## Brian Donohue

Brian is a technical CEO living in Brooklyn, NY. He owns and operates [Instapaper](https://instapaper.com), and is currently exploring new products, ideas, and spaces.

Previously, Brian held Engineering Director positions at Pinterest and DoorDash, where he led organizations of 80 people. Before that, Brian was an entrepreneur based in NYC. A chapter which began with a failed startup and ended with selling Instapaper to Pinterest.

Along the way, he [spun Instapaper out of Pinterest](https://blog.instapaper.com/post/175953870856), and now [runs Instapaper full-time](https://bthdonohue.com/2024/03/06/going-fulltime-on-instapaper.html) again.
